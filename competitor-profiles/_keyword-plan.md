# Keyword plan — alternative and comparison pages

Source: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, US, 2026-09-30. Volumes are monthly. Difficulty (KD) is 0–100.

## Finding
Nobody searches "amphetamine alternative" (0–10/mo). People search the competitor's name plus "mac" and then discover it's abandoned, paid, or limited. So each page targets the **head term** ("amphetamine mac", "caffeine mac", "caffeinate mac") and positions Midnight Oil as the alternative inside it, rather than chasing the near-zero "alternative" phrasing. Low KD across the board.
