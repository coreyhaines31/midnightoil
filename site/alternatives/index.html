Alternatives

Every way to keep a Mac awake, compared honestly.

Amphetamine, Caffeine, KeepingYouAwake, the caffeinate command, and the mouse jiggler you were about to buy. What each one does well, where it stops, and when Midnight Oil is the better choice. Sometimes it isn't; we say so.

AmphetamineThe former default. Frozen since 2023; closed-lid mode needs a three-part install. CaffeineThe 2006 original. One-click and tiny, with no timers, triggers, or closed-lid mode. caffeinate commandBuilt in and free. No lid support, no status, dies with the terminal. KeepingYouAwakeMaintained, minimal, open source. No closed-lid mode, by design. Mouse jigglersFake input to look active. Detectable, and useless with the lid closed.

How we compare

Every page here was researched from the competitor's own App Store listing, GitHub repository, release history, and user reviews as of September 2026, and each states plainly what the other tool does better. If you spot something out of date, open an issue and we'll fix it.

The short version of the category: Amphetamine has the most features and hasn't shipped since 2023. KeepingYouAwake is maintained and minimal, and won't keep a closed lid awake on principle. Caffeine is the 2006 original, a cup you click. caffeinate is built in and lives in the terminal. Mouse jigglers solve a different problem than most people think. Midnight Oil is free and open source like the best of them, and built for the way Macs get kept awake now: overnight, unattended, sometimes closed.
