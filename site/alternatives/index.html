How we compare

Every page here was researched from the competitor's own App Store listing, GitHub repository, release history, and user reviews as of September to November 2026, and each states plainly what the other tool does better. If you spot something out of date, open an issue and we'll fix it.

The short version of the category: Amphetamine has the most features and hasn't shipped since 2023. KeepingYouAwake is maintained and minimal, and won't keep a closed lid awake on principle. Caffeine is the 2006 original, a cup you click. caffeinate is built in and lives in the terminal. Mouse jigglers solve a different problem than most people think. InsomniaX, the old closed-lid favorite, hasn't run on a current Mac in years. Awake is the newest, an open-source closed-lid tool for AI agents whose menu bar app needs Apple Silicon and macOS 26. Midnight Oil is free, with its source on GitHub like the best of them, and built for the way Macs get kept awake now: overnight, unattended, sometimes closed.
