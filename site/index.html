Install Midnight Oil, a free macOS menu bar app that keeps my Mac awake (https://github.com/coreyhaines31/midnightoil):
1. Check this Mac runs macOS 14 or later with `sw_vers -productVersion`. Stop and tell me if it doesn't.
2. If Homebrew is installed (`command -v brew`), run `brew install --cask coreyhaines31/tap/midnightoil`.
3. Otherwise, download the latest release and copy it to Applications:
   url=$(curl -fsSL https://api.github.com/repos/coreyhaines31/midnightoil/releases/latest | grep -o 'https://[^"]*\.dmg' | head -1)
   curl -fsSL -o /tmp/MidnightOil.dmg "$url"
   mnt=$(hdiutil attach -nobrowse -readonly /tmp/MidnightOil.dmg | grep -o '/Volumes/.*' | tail -1)
   cp -R "$mnt/Midnight Oil.app" /Applications/ && hdiutil detach -quiet "$mnt" && rm /tmp/MidnightOil.dmg
4. Run `open -a "Midnight Oil"`, then confirm it's running with `pgrep -x "Midnight Oil"`.
5. Tell me to look for the clock icon in my menu bar, that closed-lid mode needs a one-time approval in Settings › Closed Lid, and that from now on I can just ask you to keep my Mac awake. You'll do it with the `midnightoil` command (see `midnightoil --help`; if it isn't on the PATH, it's at "/Applications/Midnight Oil.app/Contents/MacOS/midnightoil").

Your Mac sleeps.
Your agents stop.

macOS puts a Mac to sleep after a few idle minutes, and an agent that's mid-task doesn't count as activity. The terminal freezes, the SSH session drops, and you come back to half a job. Midnight Oil fixes that, whichever way you work.

Overnight

Start the run at 11. Wake up to a finished PR.

Set “Keep awake until 7 AM” before bed. The refactor, the migration, the 400 test fixes: done while you sleep, and your Mac goes back to normal at seven.

The errand

Step out for forty minutes. It keeps going.

Lunch, the school run, a workout. Tie the session to Terminal or Cursor and walk away. When the agent's app quits, the session ends on its own.

On the go

Lid closed, in the bag, still working.

Check “Stay awake with lid closed,” shut the laptop, and get on the train. The agent doesn't notice. If you're on battery, an alarm tells you it's still running.

Work hours, every week

Schedules keep your Mac awake at the same times every week: 9 to 5 on weekdays, or every night while your agents run. Add “only while plugged in” and it pauses when you unplug, then picks back up. Taking a day off? Skip it from the menu.

Just tell your agent.

Midnight Oil comes with a midnightoil command, so Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor can keep your Mac awake for you. Ask in plain words. Your Mac stays awake for exactly as long as the work takes, lid closed included, then sleeps again on its own.

  • “Keep my Mac awake until 7am.”midnightoil start --until 7am
  • “Run the tests and keep the Mac awake till they're done. I'm closing the lid.”midnightoil run --lid-closed -- npm test
  • “Stay awake for two hours, but let the screen go dark.”midnightoil start --for 2h --allow-display-sleep

Safe by design.

Keeping a laptop awake with the lid shut is the one thing macOS won't let a normal app do. Here's how Midnight Oil does it without ever leaving your Mac stuck awake.

1

A helper you approve once

macOS asks you to allow it in System Settings. It runs Apple's own pmset command and accepts instructions only from the signed Midnight Oil app.

2

Sleep always comes back

When the session ends, when the app quits, and even if the app crashes. The helper notices the dropped connection within a second and restores normal sleep.

3

Even if the helper dies

macOS restarts it, and the first thing it does on launch is restore sleep. Nothing is ever left disabled behind your back.

Questions

Which AI agents does it work with?

All of them. Midnight Oil keeps the Mac itself awake, so it doesn't matter what's running: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, a cron job, a render, or a download. Tie a session to the app that runs your agent and it ends when that app quits.

Can I just tell my agent to keep my Mac awake?

Yes. Ask Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor something like “keep my Mac awake until the migration's done, I'm closing the lid.” It runs the midnightoil command that comes with the app, and your Mac stays awake until the job finishes. Add one line to CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md and it does this for every long job without being asked. How it works.

Is it really free?

Yes, at home or at work. The whole source is on GitHub. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

Can it stay awake only during work hours?

Yes. Add a schedule like Monday to Friday, 9 to 5, and your Mac stays awake during those hours every week. Add “only while plugged in” and it pauses when you unplug and picks back up when you plug in. Taking a day off? Skip it from the menu.

Won't it drain my battery?

No. On battery, sessions end on their own below 10%, so the Mac can sleep before it runs flat. Change the level or end sessions when you unplug in Settings. Plugged in, there's nothing to worry about.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

How is this different from Amphetamine?

Same idea, and everything Amphetamine could do is here. Amphetamine stopped being updated in 2023; Midnight Oil is maintained, its source is on GitHub, and you can drag its icon around the menu bar.

Does it update itself?

Yes. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, and checks for updates on its own. You can turn that off in General settings.

Does it keep the screen on too?

Only if you want it to. “Allow display sleep” keeps the Mac awake but lets the screen turn off, which is usually what you want for an overnight run.

What if I close the lid and forget it's running?

On battery, an alarm sounds when the lid closes during a closed-lid session, and the session ends below 10% battery so the Mac can sleep. On power, it just keeps working.

Does it need admin rights?

Only closed-lid mode does, and only once: you approve its helper in System Settings › Login Items. Everything else runs as a normal app.

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

GitHub Issues. Pull requests are welcome too.
