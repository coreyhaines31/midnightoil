Everything Amphetamine did. And then some.

Midnight Oil was built to replace Amphetamine, which stopped being updated in 2023. Same ideas, a cleaner app, and the source is right there.

Triggers

Stay awake automatically while conditions hold: your office Wi-Fi, an external display, a running app, a schedule, battery level, and more. Conditions combine.

Closed-lid mode

Keep a MacBook running with the lid shut. Normal sleep comes back the moment the session ends.

Sessions your way

Indefinitely, 5 minutes to 12 hours, until a time, while an app runs, or while a download finishes. Extend without restarting.

Battery-aware

End sessions when you unplug or the battery gets low. An alarm sounds if the lid closes on battery, so nothing cooks in a bag.

Statistics

How long your Mac's been kept awake, this week and all time, and what started each session.

Safe by design.

Keeping a laptop awake with the lid shut is the one thing macOS won't let a normal app do. Here's how Midnight Oil does it without ever leaving your Mac in a bad state.

1

A helper you approve once

macOS asks you to allow it in System Settings. It runs Apple's own pmset command and accepts instructions only from the signed Midnight Oil app.

2

Sleep always comes back

When the session ends, when the app quits, and even if the app crashes. The helper notices the dropped connection within a second and restores normal sleep.

3

Even if the helper dies

macOS restarts it, and the first thing it does on launch is restore sleep. Nothing is ever left disabled behind your back.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It's MIT-licensed and the whole thing is on GitHub. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Can I move the icon in the menu bar?

Yes. Hold ⌘ and drag it wherever you like. It remembers.

Does it update itself?

Yes. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, and checks for updates on its own. You can turn that off in General settings.

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

GitHub Issues. Pull requests are welcome too.
