Keep your Mac awake.

A free, open-source menu bar app that stops your Mac from sleeping. For long renders, big downloads, presentations, and everything else that shouldn't be interrupted.

Everything Amphetamine did. Still maintained.

Midnight Oil was built as a replacement for Amphetamine, which stopped being updated in 2023. Same ideas, cleaner app, and the source is right there.

Sessions your way

Indefinitely, for 5 minutes to 12 hours, until a time, while an app runs, or while a download finishes. Extend without restarting.

Closed-lid mode

Keep a MacBook running with the lid shut. A small helper handles it and always restores normal sleep afterwards, even if something crashes.

Triggers

Stay awake automatically on your office Wi-Fi, when a display is plugged in, while Final Cut is open, on a schedule, and more. Conditions combine.

Battery-aware

End sessions when you unplug or when the battery gets low. An alarm sounds if the lid closes on battery so nothing cooks in a bag.

Drive Alive

Keep external drives from spinning down during a session, or all the time.

Hot keys, icons, stats

Global shortcuts, your own menu bar icons, notification sounds, and a record of how long your Mac's been kept awake.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's MIT-licensed and the whole thing is on GitHub. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

How is closed-lid mode safe?

macOS won't let a normal app keep a laptop awake with the lid shut, so Midnight Oil uses a helper you approve once in System Settings. It disables lid sleep only while a closed-lid session runs, and restores it when the session ends, when the app quits, or if the app crashes. If the helper itself dies, macOS restarts it and it restores sleep on launch.

Does it work on Apple Silicon and Intel?

Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Can I move the icon in the menu bar?

Yes. Hold ⌘ and drag it wherever you like. It remembers.

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

GitHub Issues. Pull requests are welcome too.
